## Contribution and scope

Karpathy describes an end-to-end generated app and integration difficulties; this is experience, not a controlled productivity estimate. METR's early-2025 randomized study involved 16 experienced developers and 246 tasks in familiar repositories, finding a 19% completion-time increase in that setting. Its February 2026 follow-up reports selection and measurement problems that make later estimates hard to interpret. Neither result establishes a universal effect for all developers or tools. GitHub's application card documents agent capabilities and limitations rather than independently proving productivity.

## Reading route

Separate anecdote, controlled observation, and vendor documentation. For your workflow measure elapsed time, correction effort, verified quality, and maintenance. Revisit evidence as tools and tasks change; the graph does not rank current products.
