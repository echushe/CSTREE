## Contribution and scope

An operations-oriented source for service objectives, monitoring, releases, overload, and critical state. Its examples connect software design with production evidence and recovery. The techniques reflect a particular operational setting and should be scaled to actual service needs.

## Reading route

Start with service-level objectives and monitoring. Follow one request through dependency failure, overload, and recovery. Read critical-state and data-integrity chapters alongside the consensus and storage notes.
